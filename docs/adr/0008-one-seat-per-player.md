# One seat per player; unseated presence lasts only while connected

A player holds at most one seat in all rooms. They keep it while they visit other pages, until they leave the room. Taking a seat in another room frees the old one, and the server refuses it while the old room has a running match. A person without a seat (a spectator) is in a room only while connected to it, so they can watch any number of rooms. While a player is seated in a running match, every page outside their room shows a modal that lets them only return or abandon. While they are seated with no match running, every page shows a way back to their room. We chose this over free multi-room membership: a match pauses while a seated player is away, and three people should not wait for someone who is browsing elsewhere in the app.
